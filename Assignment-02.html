College Terminology

Syllabus
An outline of the subjects in a course of study.
GPA
Grade Point Average, a standard measure of scholastic achievement.
Prerequisite
A course that a student must pass before enrolling in a more advanced class.
Credits
Units used to measure the academic workload and progress toward a degree.
Auditorium
A large building or hall used for public gatherings, lectures, and performances.
